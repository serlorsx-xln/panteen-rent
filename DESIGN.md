# Design System: Panteen Rent

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The White Room"**

A white room and black ink, and the photographs carry all the colour. The storefront behaves like an international luxury retailer: edge-to-edge photographs, a great deal of air, Thai set large and light, nothing boxed and nothing coloured in. What it does that those retailers do not is ask for the event date first, and answer in words on every dress whether it is free that day.

Density is generous on the storefront (one gutter for everything, 64 to 128px between blocks) and tight in the back office, which keeps the same tokens and type on a quiet warm-grey desk and allows status tones, boxed panels and dense tables. Depth is flat: hairlines, one warm paper tile and the near-black footer do the work; shadows belong only to things that float.

Two earlier looks were rejected by the client and are not to return: a social-feed storefront (profile header, story discs, square photo grid) and a "flagship directory" on stone with a mulberry slab and ruled boxes.

**Key Characteristics:**
- White ground, black ink, no brand colour; the photographs are the colour.
- Anuphan Thai at weight 200 to 300 for display, 400 for reading; Libre Caslon Display for the wordmark and the figures that matter.
- Everything cut square; circles only where a thing is truly round (dots, avatars).
- Availability said in words with a filled or an open dot; a taken dress greys out.
- The date desk: a ruled row of cells that is the search, the filter and the booking control.
- Slow, small motion that reduced-motion removes entirely.

## Colors

A neutral warm-grey ladder from white to near-black; colour enters only through photographs and, on working screens, status tones.

### Primary
- **Gallery Ink** (ink): every action, every rule that matters (the desk's top rule, the total's rules, the verdict rule), selected states, the search cell. Ink is the only "accent".

### Neutral
- **White Room** (ground / surface): the page and every surface on it.
- **Paper Tile** (sunk): the tile behind every photograph, hover on desk cells and menus, the booking summary column, the one quiet band (the itemised-price section).
- **Second Ink** (ink-2): secondary text, ledes, muted (taken) prices and names.
- **Third Ink** (ink-3): labels, meta lines, captions; the quietest text that still reads on white and on the paper tile.
- **Hairline** (line): dividers between cells, rows and sections.
- **Soft Rule** (line-soft): rules between rows inside a list or price strip.
- **Control Edge** (line-2): the edge of an input, tick box or switch at rest.
- **Near Black** (black): the demo strip and the footer only, with Warm Paper Text (on-black), Warm Grey Text (on-black-2) and white rules at 18% (line-black).
- **Scrim** (scrim): behind the phone filter drawer, the off-canvas admin nav and sheets.
- **Back-office Desk** (desk-admin): the admin page ground under white sheets.

### Status (working screens only)
- **OK** (ok / ok-bg), **Wait** (wait / wait-bg), **Bad** (bad / bad-bg), **Live** (live / live-bg), **Done** (ink-2 on line-soft): booking states, flash messages, field errors, the countdown, the fit note icon. Bad also marks destructive buttons and invalid fields on the storefront.

### Named Rules
**The Photographs-Carry-Colour Rule.** No brand colour, no tinted panels, no coloured bars. If a hue appears on the storefront that is not inside a photograph or a status message, it is wrong.

**The Status-Stays-At-Work Rule.** Status tones live on booking, payment, account and admin screens. They never decorate the catalogue, the dress cards or the home page.

**The Black-Is-A-Floor Rule.** Near-black is the demo strip and the footer. It is never a section background, a card or a banner mid-page.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Anuphan (self-hosted Thai and Latin subsets, weights 200 to 700; falls back to Noto Sans Thai, system-ui)
**Body Font:** Anuphan at 400
**Wordmark / Numerals:** Libre Caslon Display 400 (self-hosted Latin subset; Thai falls through to Anuphan)

**Character:** A light, open Thai sans set very large and very thin against a high-contrast Didone-like serif used sparingly for the name and the numbers, the way a fashion house sets a price.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (200, hero at the clamp in `display`, 1.22): the two-line hero headline only; phones use `display-phone` at 300.
- **Headline** (200 to 300): listing titles (`headline-listing`), page titles (`headline-page`), section heads on the home page (`headline-section`).
- **Title** (300): the dress title, section heads on the dress page, working-panel heads; 400 to 500 for small heads (calendar month, filter drawer).
- **Desk value** (300, 19 to 24px): the chosen date, category and size inside a desk cell; reads like a filled-in form.
- **Body** (400, 16px, 1.7; 15.5px under 720px): reading text, prose at 46 to 64ch.
- **Label** (500 or 600, 13.5 to 14px): field labels, filter-group legends. Labels sit directly above their control; they are never free-floating eyebrows.
- **Caption** (400, 12.5 to 13px, ink-3): meta lines, desk labels, B.E. date readouts, legends.
- **Numerals** (Libre Caslon Display 400, tabular): price on the dress (42px), totals (34px), the listing count (28px, 24px on phones), the itemised band's figures, the step numbers, the amount due, the error code, admin counts.

### Named Rules
**The Light-At-Size Rule.** The larger the Thai, the thinner: 200 at headline sizes, 300 for titles and desk values, 400 for reading, 500 to 600 only for small labels and emphasis.

**The Serif-For-Name-And-Number Rule.** Libre Caslon Display sets the wordmark (letter-spaced capitals, 0.14 to 0.2em) and the figures that decide. It never sets Thai, a sentence or a button.

**The Tabular Rule.** Prices, counts, dates and table cells use tabular figures.

## Layout

One gutter for everything (`--gutter`, 20 to 56px fluid) inside a 1760px page (`--maxw`); working pages use the 1280px column (`--col`). The top bar is 72px (60px at 1079px and below). Above 1761px the date desk pads itself to line up with the page instead of the window.

- **Home:** edge-to-edge hero photograph (about 730px tall at 1440 by 900) with the bar floating over it, then the four-cell date desk flush to both edges, then open blocks spaced 64 to 128px.
- **Listing:** title, the deskbar (date, what the date means, the free-only switch), then a 236px filter rail beside the card grid. One count only, beside the sort.
- **Dress:** the photograph fills the left half edge to edge; the booking box (max 500px) stands centred in the right half. Below, calendar on the left, details on the right.
- **Booking:** form column plus a 340 to 420px sticky summary column on the paper tile, from 900px.
- **Cards:** four across on home, three beside the rail (four from 1680px), two on phones; row gap 36 to 60px, column gap 12 to 24px.

Breakpoints as shipped: 1279px (nav folds into a full-screen sheet), 1079px (60px bar, smaller wordmark, filter rail becomes a right-hand drawer), 899px (phone: hero at about 60svh, desk stacked, two-up cards, dress page single column, deskbar reduced to date and switch), 719px, 560px (forms single column). Two height rules: at 1080px+ wide and 853px or shorter, the filter rail stops being sticky and scrolls with the page; at 1080px+ wide and 1000px+ tall, the dress booking box becomes sticky.

## Elevation & Depth

Flat. Surfaces at rest have no shadow and no card border; depth comes from the paper tile, hairlines and the near-black footer. The top bar gets a 1px hairline (drawn as `0 1px 0` line) once the page moves.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Pop** (`0 24px 48px -28px rgba(19,17,16,.34), 0 6px 14px -8px rgba(19,17,16,.14)`): the account menu, the toast, the phone filter drawer, the off-canvas admin nav.
- **Sheet** (`0 -20px 48px -26px rgba(19,17,16,.36)`): bottom sheets on phones.

### Named Rules
**The Only-Floating-Things Rule.** A shadow means the thing is above the page. Cards, panels, tiles, desk cells and tables never cast one.

## Shapes

Everything is cut square: radius 0 for buttons, inputs, chips, cards, photographs, panels, switches and tick boxes. Circles appear only where the thing is round by nature: availability dots, the status dot, avatars, the favourite button over a photograph, the calendar's today mark, colour swatches. Photographs are 2:3 for dresses, 3:4 for occasions, cropped high (object-position about 50% 18%). Disclosures use a drawn chevron, never the browser triangle.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Shape:** square (0px).
- **Primary:** ink fill, white text, 1px ink border, 50px tall, 10px 28px padding, Anuphan 500 15px. Sizes: sm 40px, xs 32px, lg 58px.
- **Hover / Focus:** inverts to white with ink text over 0.25s; 2px ink outline offset 3px on focus.
- **Ghost:** transparent with ink border; hover fills ink. **Danger:** bad-coloured outline; hover fills bad.
- **Loading:** label goes transparent and a thin ring spins in its place.

### The Date Desk (signature)
A row of square cells divided by hairlines, each a small label (13px, ink-3) over a large light value (300). On home: four cells (date widest, category, size, then the ink search cell with an arrow that slides 7px on hover), 96px tall, flush to the page edges, hover tints to the paper tile. On the listing (deskbar): ink top rule, date cell, a sentence giving the pickup and return days, then the free-only switch and a clear-date link. On the dress page and in the booking form the same date control sits on a single ink or line rule. With scripts the date reads in the Thai Buddhist calendar while the native picker sits invisibly over the cell. Choosing a date re-lights the grid in place: cards fade up in a 38ms stagger with their verdict, the count changes, nothing reloads.

### Dress Cards (vit)
The photograph on the paper tile, then three quiet lines with nothing drawn around them: an availability line (13px, filled 7px dot, "ว่าง" with the date) when a date is set, the name (15.5px, two lines max), a size/colour meta line (ink-3), the price (500, tabular, with "/ 3 วัน" in ink-3). A taken dress: photograph greyscale at 75% opacity, open dot, name and price in ink-2 at 400. The photograph eases 4% larger under the hand (hover-capable devices only); a round favourite button appears on hover.

### Filter Rail
236px, sticky under the bar; groups divided by hairlines with 13.5px 600 legends. Category links show counts in ink-3. Sizes and every other choice are words beside a 16px square tick: empty box at rest, an ink square inside when chosen (box-shadow inset ring, not a check glyph). Colour and price fold away behind drawn chevrons. With scripts choices apply themselves and the action buttons hide; removable chips appear above the grid. Under 1080px the rail becomes a right-hand drawer (max 420px) over the scrim with a sticky footer carrying the result count.

### The Switch
Square: a 40 by 22px ink-outlined track with a 14px square knob; on, the track fills ink and the knob turns white and slides 18px. It keeps only the free dresses and is hidden on phones until a date is chosen.

### Verdict
The answer on the dress page: an ink top rule, 19px text, a 9px filled dot for free. Taken: line rule, ink-2 text, an open dot, and a sub-line pointing to free days or other free dresses. No date: line rule, 17px prompt, no dot.

### Price Strip
Every line of money before commitment: rows separated by soft rules, the amount at 500 and right-aligned, a small ink-3 note under each. The total sits between two ink rules with the figure in the serif at 34px. **Muted (taken date):** a caption says which days the prices are for, rows turn ink-2 at 400, the total's rules drop to line and the figure shrinks to 24px.

### Panel
On the storefront a working section stands open under a single hairline with a light 21 to 26px title; only the booking summary column sits on the paper tile. In the back office panels are boxed white sheets with a line border and a 17px 500 title, so a screen of them scans as separate jobs.

### Chips
Square, 40px (34px above the grid), 1px line border, 14px; hover border ink; on: ink fill, white text. Used for applied filters and the account sub-navigation (where they become underlined tabs).

### Inputs / Fields
- **Style:** square, 1px control-edge border, 50px tall, 16px text. In the booking form and the rail search, fields are underlines only (bottom border, no side padding).
- **Focus:** border goes ink plus a 1px ink ring (underline fields: a second 1px ink line below).
- **Error / Disabled:** bad border and ring with a bad message; disabled on the paper tile with ink-3 text.

### Navigation
The 72px bar: categories left (14.5px, an underline draws in from the left on hover and for the current page), the letter-spaced serif wordmark centred, account links right. Over the home photograph it is transparent with white type until the page moves, then white with a hairline. At 1279px and below the categories fold into a full-screen sheet with 26px 300 entries and counts.

### Footer
Near-black, the wordmark set very large (up to 96px, 0.14em), a four-column link grid under a white 18% rule, legal line in 12.5px on-black-2.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** keep the ground white and let the photographs carry every hue; the paper tile (#F4F2EF) is the only tint on the storefront.
- **Do** say availability in words with a filled (free) or open (taken) dot, and grey out a taken dress's photograph.
- **Do** set the wordmark and decisive figures in Libre Caslon Display with tabular numerals; set all Thai in Anuphan, lighter as it gets larger.
- **Do** reuse the date desk for every date entry: home search, listing deskbar, dress page, booking form.
- **Do** use one gutter (`--gutter`) for every edge and one hairline for every division.

### Don't:
- **Don't** round corners: no radius on buttons, inputs, chips, cards, panels or photographs. Circles only for things that are round.
- **Don't** put shadows on anything at rest; shadows are for menus, toasts, drawers and sheets.
- **Don't** draw borders around product cards or storefront sections.
- **Don't** use gradient text.
- **Don't** put eyebrow or kicker labels above headings.
- **Don't** use a coloured side border as an accent stripe on cards, alerts or rows.
- **Don't** introduce a brand colour, a tinted section or a solid colour bar.
- **Don't** signal availability by colour alone.
- **Don't** bring back the rejected looks: the social-feed storefront or the stone-and-mulberry ruled directory.
